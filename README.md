# SCIP Study Hub — SAVIGA-C01

Interactive study hub for the **Saviynt Certified IGA Professional** exam.

**Live site:** https://mkheira1923.github.io/scip-study-hub/

## What's in it

- **177 questions** with explanations and a source on every one
- **150 flashcards** across the six exam domains
- **Timed mock exam** — 70 questions drawn to the official 15 / 15 / 10 / 10 / 15 / 5 weighting, 2-hour clock, 50–500 scoring with a 350 pass line, per-domain breakdown
- **Study pages** per domain: objectives, UI paths, job names, and the traps that cost people marks
- **Saviynt ↔ SailPoint bridge** — 40 mappings for anyone crossing over
- Glossary, sources, light/dark, works offline, progress saved in your browser

## The blueprint

The official Content Outline is six domains totalling exactly 70 questions:

| # | Domain | Questions |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Building Identity Warehouse | 15 |
| 02 | Access Request System (ARS) | 15 |
| 03 | Rules and Policies | 10 |
| 04 | Segregation of Duties (SoDs) | 10 |
| 05 | Attestation | 15 |
| 06 | Analytics | 5 |
| | **Total** | **70** |

Verified against the published [Saviynt SCIP Exam Preparation Guide](https://saviynt.com/hubfs/Saviynt%20Certified%20IGA%20Professional%20(SCIP)%20Exam%20Preparation%20Guide%20(1).pdf?hsLang=en).

## Sources

Built from publicly available Saviynt documentation — the Exam Preparation Guide and the
[EIC Administration Guide](https://docs.saviyntcloud.com/). Items from Saviynt's partner/customer-only
training materials are deliberately excluded from this public edition.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Saviynt. Study aid only.

## Running it

It's one self-contained HTML file with no dependencies and no network calls. Open `index.html` in any browser,
or serve the folder with anything static.
